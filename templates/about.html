📐 Geotechnical Engineering Foundations

Rock Mechanics Principles

Slope instability and rockfalls are governed by the Mohr-Coulomb Failure Criterion and the Limit Equilibrium Factor of Safety (FS):

\(\tau = c + (\sigma - u) \tan(\phi)\)  |  \(FS = \frac{\text{Resisting Shear Strength } \tau}{\text{Driving Gravitational & Inertial Stress } \tau_d}\)
  • Rainfall & Soil Moisture: Prolonged rainfall elevates pore-water pressure (\(u\)), directly reducing effective normal stress (\(\sigma' = \sigma - u\)) and shear strength along pre-existing rock joints.
  • Ground Vibration (PPV): Dynamic shockwaves induce transient inertial acceleration (\(k_h \cdot W\)), shifting the factor of safety below unity (\(FS < 1.0\)) and detaching hanging rock wedges.
  • Displacement Rate & Creep Velocity: Accelerating strain displacement (\(\frac{dd}{dt} > 15 \text{ mm/day}\)) characterizes tertiary creep, signaling that micro-cracks have coalesced into a continuous catastrophic slip surface.

🎓 University Viva Voce Preparation Guide

Examiner Q&A Alignment
Q1: Why use an independent Rule-Based Backstop in addition to Machine Learning?

Answer: Supervised ML models are inductive and probabilistic; during unexpected sensor anomalies or out-of-distribution inputs, an ML model could theoretically predict 'Low Risk' due to feature counterbalancing. Because rockfalls pose immediate life-safety hazards, the rule-based backstop acts as a deterministic safety constraint that cannot be overridden by ML probabilities.

Q2: How does the system prevent hallucinated or dangerous evacuation instructions?

Answer: The emergency coordination module uses deterministic document grounding. It retrieves verified evacuation routes, muster points, and incident commander contacts strictly from administrator-uploaded SOPs stored in SQLite. If a zone lacks a verified SOP, it refuses to fabricate routes and explicitly instructs responders to follow official emergency civil defense lines (112).

Q3: How was data leakage prevented during model training?

Answer: Data preprocessing used a strict 80/20 stratified split. The feature standardizer (StandardScaler) was fitted exclusively on the 80% training set and then applied to transform both the test set and production inference requests. 5-Fold Stratified Cross Validation was conducted solely within the training partition.
